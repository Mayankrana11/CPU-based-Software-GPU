### DOOM Setup (Optional)

To experiment with DOOM compatibility, create a `doom/` directory in this folder and place your DOOM WAD & EXE files (`DOOM.WAD`),  (`DOOM.EXE`) and Chocolate Doom installation. Keep these files local and **do not commit or redistribute them** with this repository.

```text
root/
└── game/
    └── doom/
        ├── DOOM.WAD
        ├── DOOM.EXE
        └── chocolate-doom files
```
